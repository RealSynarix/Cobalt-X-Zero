Tools you’ll need:
- Pliers (to remove supports and pluck off imperfections)
- File / sandpaper (for aesthetics and mechanical fit)

General steps for every part:
- Pluck off any stringing
- Check for faults
- Lightly sand flat areas (stay away from small features)
- Remove all support material

Bottom Shell:
- If needed, sand the inside of the lens socket so the lens part drops in cleanly

PCB-Holder:
- sand edges of pushdown clip heavily until fit (must not be loose)

Scroll Wheel:
- Sand the rough edge that appears after printing (sand it hard)
- Sand the little button pad that presses the MMB

Side Buttons:
- Sand the front faces (the parts your thumb actually touches)
